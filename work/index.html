01 / Strategic / Data intelligence

Build a clearer view of the business.

What do we need to know to make a better decision?

I start with the decision, not the dashboard. What does someone need to understand, and what would they do differently if they could? That gives the data work a purpose.

Sometimes the information is already there, scattered across systems, documents and people. Sometimes it doesn’t exist yet. I’ve worked on both: making difficult data usable, and building products and systems that collect the missing context.

Getting the data ready is only part of the work. People need to understand it, trust it and use it. Training and adoption—from the C-suite to front-line managers—belong in the delivery, not in a handover after the build.

The test: does it change a decision, and does that decision improve the business?

02 / Core operations / Applications

Build a better way to get the work done.

What could this operation deliver if its systems stopped holding it back?

I’m especially interested in the work at the heart of a business: planning capacity, running production, coordinating delivery or providing the service customers pay for. Often, important parts still live in Excel, with people moving information between spreadsheets and systems that were never designed to work together.

That isn’t just an administrative inconvenience. It can limit how much the business can deliver, how reliably it keeps its promises and whether it can grow without adding cost at the same rate. Those are the applications I want to build—where better tools can materially change the value the business creates.

I start with the people doing the work. Where does it stall? What gets done twice? Where does quality depend on someone working around a system that doesn’t fit? Sometimes the answer is connecting what’s already there. Sometimes it’s building a product from zero to one. I’ve done both, across web and desktop applications and internal tools.

I want to carry the work into production and daily use, then learn from what happens. Integration, adoption and feedback are part of making the product work, not extras after an MVP.

The test: can the business deliver more, deliver it better and grow without the operation becoming the constraint?
