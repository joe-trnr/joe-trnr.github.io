02 / The kind of work I love

Build a business
that can do more.

I’m most interested in the work that changes what a business is capable of: how it grows, how well it delivers, and how effectively its people can work.

01Top-line growthGive the business more room to grow.+

I love finding what a business could offer that it can’t today. A new product or service, a better customer experience, or a capability that makes the next stage of growth possible.

02Core operationsMake the business better at what it does.+

How a company delivers matters as much as what it sells. I’m interested in improving the quality, reliability and effectiveness of the operation itself—work that can strengthen margins and support growth together.

03Back officeHelp the people doing the work.+

The back office should enable the business—not cripple it by tying people up in low-value work. I’d judge the work by time freed up, costs kept in proportion and what it enables the rest of the business to do.
